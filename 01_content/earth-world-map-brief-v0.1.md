candidate generation. This brief is planning only. It does not approve image

## Selected Direction

Earth region world map display direction:

- Region: `翠瀑環谷`
- Town hub: `翠瀑湖城`
- Minor dungeon A: `古根外環`
- Minor dungeon B: `瀑崖採石場`
- Main dungeon: `地脈石城`
- Main phase 1: `外城遺構`
- Main phase 2: `深脈殿`

The Earth world map should feel like a complete natural region, not only a
forest. It includes canyon landforms, cliffs, a major waterfall, a lake, rivers,
ancient roots, quarry faces, mineral veins, and a half-buried leyline ruin.

## World Map Composition

Match the broad readability of the Ice world map visual reference:

- High-angle fantasy world-map view, not a parchment map.
- Clear routes and node readability from the town hub to each major location.
- `翠瀑湖城` sits near the center beside the lake.
- `古根外環` appears in the foreground or near-side route as an ancient root
  and ruin-ring area.
- `瀑崖採石場` appears on a side route near cliffs, exposed stone, and the
  large waterfall.
- `地脈石城` appears in the far route as a half-buried entrance into the earth,
  more like a sealed ancient ruin than a fully exposed city.
- Roads, bridges, river crossings, or terraced paths should show the progression
  from lake city to outer root ruins, quarry cliffs, and the deep leyline site.

Do not put readable text labels inside the image. Region and location names
belong in UI, documents, or candidate tables, not in the generated map image.

## Visual Mood

Target mood:

- Mysterious but livable.
- Rain-cleared light after wet weather.
- Water, rock, moss, roots, city lights, quarry stone, and leyline ruins all
  remain visually legible.
- The area should not feel abandoned, apocalyptic, snowy, volcanic, desert-like,
  or like a plain forest.

Color and material variety should come from the landforms themselves:

- Waterfall spray, river paths, and the central lake.
- Pale and warm rock faces around cliffs and quarry cuts.
- Green moss, old roots, and fungus glow as accents.
- Compact lake-town buildings with warm lights.
- Deeper mineral or leyline glow near `地脈石城`.

## Image Brief Draft

Create a high-angle fantasy world map of `翠瀑環谷`, an Earth-element region
with a compact lakeside hub city at the center. The map shows a canyon valley
with cliffs, a massive waterfall, a central leyline lake, branching rivers,
ancient roots crossing stone terraces, old quarry faces, exposed mineral veins,
and roads connecting several readable adventure nodes. The central town,
`翠瀑湖城`, should look like a real regional hub but remain compact and integrated
with the lake shore, bridges, docks, and surrounding roots.

In the foreground or near-side route, show `古根外環` as an ancient ring of giant
roots and broken stonework. On a side route near the waterfall cliffs, show
`瀑崖採石場` as an old quarry cut into wet rock and mineral veins. In the distant
route, show `地脈石城` as a half-buried ancient leyline city entrance descending
into the earth, with visible outer ruins and a deeper sealed temple glow
suggesting `外城遺構` and `深脈殿`.

Lighting should be clear after rain, with mist from the waterfall, readable
paths, warm town lights, moss and fungus accents, varied rock colors, flowing
water, and a mysterious but livable atmosphere. The image should have no text
labels, no watermarks, no UI overlay, and no unreadable generated writing.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid:

- Text labels or map lettering inside the image.
- A small garden-like town hub.
- A plain forest-only map.
- Snowfield, icy coast, lava, desert, or storm-tower identity.
- A fully exposed giant main castle that competes with Final-region scale.
- A ruined wasteland tone that makes the hub feel uninhabitable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The image reads as Earth at first glance, distinct from Fire, Ice, Thunder,
  and Final.
- `翠瀑湖城` reads as a later-region town hub, not a small village or private
  garden.
- The route structure is legible: lake city to root-ring ruins, quarry cliffs,
  and half-buried leyline stone city.
- Canyon, cliff, waterfall, lake, river, root, quarry, and ancient leyline ruin
  elements are all present without making the scene visually cluttered.
- No visible text labels, watermarks, UI overlays, snowfield theme, desert theme,
  lava theme, or one-note green forest treatment.
