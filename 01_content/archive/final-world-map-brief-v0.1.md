# Final World Map Brief v0.1

Purpose: record the selected Final region world-map direction for future visual
candidate generation. This brief is planning only. It does not approve image
generation, GUI implementation, runtime data edits, quest text edits, schema
changes, save changes, combat changes, asset-pipeline work, staging, or
commits.

## Selected Direction

Final region world map display direction:

- Region: `魔王城前線`
- Town hub: `終門要塞`
- Hub tone: adventurer coalition fortress.
- Minor dungeon A: `攻城外壕`
- Minor dungeon B: `破封城墟`
- Main dungeon: `黑曜王城`
- Main phase 1: `黑曜城門`
- Main phase 2: `封核中樞`
- Main phase 3: `魔王王座`

The Final world map should not present a fifth element or a four-element echo
showcase. It is the Demon King's territory: a night-colored final approach where
gathered adventurers challenge the black fortress city from an established
front-line base.

## World Map Composition

Match the broad readability of the Ice world map visual reference:

- High-angle fantasy world-map view, not a parchment map.
- Clear routes and node readability from the town hub to each major location.
- `黑曜王城` sits near the center as the main visual endpoint.
- `終門要塞` sits to the side, still large enough to read as the final region's
  town hub and adventurer coalition base.
- `攻城外壕` appears on the first approach route as an outer moat, trench,
  broken approach line, or defended gap before the enemy city.
- `破封城墟` appears on the second route as a breached ruin or broken seal-city
  outer district.
- Routes should show a final challenge path from the adventurer fortress,
  through the outer approach and ruins, up black obsidian steps and gates, into
  `黑曜城門`, toward `封核中樞`, and finally to `魔王王座`.

Do not put readable text labels inside the image. Region and location names
belong in UI, documents, or candidate tables, not in the generated map image.

## Visual Mood

Target mood:

- Black-obsidian cold night with human firelight.
- Final challenge, not kingdom-war spectacle.
- The enemy territory should feel like the Demon King's own land, not a mix of
  Fire, Ice, Earth, and Thunder motifs.
- The adventurer side should feel established after sustained attempts: not a
  small camp, not a royal army parade, and not a ruined refugee outpost.

Use night contrast and readable silhouettes:

- Black spires, obsidian walls, sharp rooftops, and a central royal-city mass.
- Cold blue-gray night and moonlit cloud layers.
- Warm campfire lines, supply tents, healer tents, guild boards, equipment
  carts, and watch platforms around `終門要塞`.
- Faint magical or sealed glow only where it supports `封核中樞`; do not turn it
  into elemental residue.

## Image Brief Draft

Create a high-angle fantasy world map of `魔王城前線`, a night-colored final
approach to the Demon King's fortress city. The map centers on `黑曜王城`, a
black spired royal city with an ominous silhouette, visible gate approach,
sealed-core direction, and distant throne ascent. It should feel like the
Demon King's own territory, with obsidian walls, sharp towers, dark rooftops,
cold night clouds, and a dangerous final route into the city.

Place `終門要塞` to the side as an adventurer coalition fortress rather than a
kingdom army base. It should still read as the final region's town hub: campfire
supply lines, healer tents, guild boards, equipment carts, supply wagons, watch
platforms, temporary defenses, and gathered adventurer parties should make it
feel like many challengers have been preparing and attempting the final push for
some time.

The route should read as a final challenge path: from the adventurer fortress,
through `攻城外壕`, across `破封城墟`, up the black obsidian steps and gates,
into `黑曜城門`, toward `封核中樞`, and finally to `魔王王座`. Lighting should
use black-obsidian cold night, blue-gray moonlit shadows, warm campfires, and
readable fortress silhouettes. The image should have no text labels, no
watermarks, no UI overlay, no unreadable generated writing, and no visible
four-element echo treatment.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid:

- Text labels or map lettering inside the image.
- Fire, Ice, Earth, or Thunder motifs as major visual echoes.
- A fifth-element palette or abstract elemental convergence.
- A kingdom-army war epic with massed banners, legions, or grand battlefield
  spectacle.
- A tiny camp that fails to read as a final town hub.
- A bright heroic daytime palette.
- A generic lava demon realm, snowy wasteland, desert ruin, or forest approach.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The image reads as Final at first glance: the adventurer side has reached the
  Demon King's territory.
- `黑曜王城` reads as the central destination, with a spired black-city
  silhouette and visible gate-to-throne escalation.
- `終門要塞` reads as an adventurer coalition fortress and later-region hub,
  even though it is placed to the side.
- The route structure is legible: adventurer fortress to outer moat, breached
  ruins, black gate, sealed core, and Demon King's throne.
- Campfires, supply lines, healer tents, guild boards, equipment carts, watch
  platforms, temporary defenses, obsidian steps, and the black spired city are
  present without making the scene visually cluttered.
- No visible text labels, watermarks, UI overlays, four-element echo showcase,
  fifth-element treatment, or kingdom-war spectacle.
